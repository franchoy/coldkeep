# v1.10.9 Phase 6 — No-Silent-Corruption Verification Matrix

Status: Complete

## Purpose

Phase 6 consolidates v1.10.9 filesystem fault-injection evidence into a release-level no-silent-corruption verification matrix.

## Core Invariant

Every v1.10.9 injected filesystem failure must either fail closed, remain retry-safe, or be detectable by verification/recovery. No injected fault may produce silent success with corrupted data.

## Inputs Reviewed

Phase 6 reviews evidence from:

- Phase 1 — scripted fault filesystem helper;
- Phase 2 — restore fault injection scenarios;
- Phase 3 — container write/seal fault injection scenarios;
- Phase 4 — recovery/quarantine fault injection scenarios;
- Phase 5 — GC delete failure scenario.

## Output

Phase 6 adds:

```text
docs/release/v1.10/v1.10.9-no-silent-corruption-matrix.md
```

This document records the surface-by-surface safety matrix for v1.10.9.

## Safety Conclusions

The covered v1.10.9 fault surfaces demonstrate:

* no false successful restore under injected restore faults;
* no corrupted final restore output reported as success;
* no false durable container success under injected container faults;
* no unsafe recovery/quarantine classification under injected recovery faults;
* GC delete failure remains retry-safe under current behavior;
* all injected faults are deterministic and test-only.

## Known Seam Gaps

Phase 6 documents known seam gaps rather than hiding them.

Known gaps include restore temp-file creation/write/sync and container rollback truncate behavior where they remain outside the `fsx.FS` seam.

These are not treated as v1.10.9 regressions because v1.10.9 is scoped to deterministic test-only fault injection on currently available seam surfaces.
