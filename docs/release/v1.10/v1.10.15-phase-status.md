## Checklist Execution Findings (Phase 5 pre-release run)

During pre-release checklist execution (Section 3 CI matrix), two pre-existing
test infrastructure bugs were found and fixed in-band:

1. `internal/maintenance/gc_step11_test.go` (6 INSERT statements) and
   `internal/maintenance/gc_test.go` (1 INSERT statement): packed-block
   `storage_blocks` fixtures were missing the `physical_hash` column, causing
   verify to fail closed with "physical_hash missing for packed block".
   Fix: added `blocks.HashPhysical(encodedBytes)` as the `physical_hash` value.

2. `tests/utils/common.go` `ResetDB()` TRUNCATE statement was missing
   `snapshot`, `snapshot_path`, `snapshot_file`, and `physical_file` tables,
   causing `TestSnapshotRetentionChurnLongRun` to fail on second codec run
   with duplicate key violations. Fix: added the 4 missing tables.

Both bugs were pre-existing on `main` before v1.10.15 changes.
