## Closure Decisions

### CK-110-M035 — accepted

Production complexity hotspots in `loadBlockMetadata` and `mapVerifyPipelineFailure` are accepted at
S2 severity. Behavior-preserving refactors are explicitly excluded from v1.10.3 fixes-only scope.
Complexity tracking is retained in the matrix. Phases 1–7 implemented and validated the relevant
correctness invariants without requiring structural refactoring.

**Closure proof:** Phase 1–7 implementation complete; packed-metadata invariants validated via phases
1–7 test matrix (v1.10.3-test-inventory.csv); all 3 source production-complexity findings accepted
with complexity tracking retained per S2 risk classification.

### CK-110-M060 — deferred

Fourteen test-helper complexity findings in `storage_block_reader_test.go` are deferred. Structural
refactoring of test helpers is outside the fixes-only boundary for v1.10.3. These findings present no
production correctness risk and have no direct evidence of critical-path impact. Issues are retained
in the backlog for post-integrity-hardening review.

**Closure proof:** 14 test-complexity findings deferred; structural refactoring of test helpers
excluded from v1.10.3 fixes-only scope; issues retained in backlog for post-integrity-hardening
review.

### CK-110-M061 — deferred

Two Opengrep/Semgrep `go/filesystem/rule-fileread` findings in `storage_block_reader_test.go` are
deferred. The flagged patterns (`os.Open`, `os.OpenFile`) are benign dynamic-path usage restricted to
test fixtures; they are not production correctness invariants. Issues are retained for post-integrity
hardening review.

**Closure proof:** 2 test-only dynamic-path scanner findings deferred; not production correctness
invariants; test file patterns confirmed benign; retained for post-integrity hardening review.
