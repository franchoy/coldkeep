## Contract decisions

### Snapshot membership

Snapshot identity and lineage were reviewed. `snapshot_file` and
`snapshot_path` membership behavior, plus show/list/diff/restore/delete and
retention consumers, were clarified as current contract surfaces. The remaining
backend-sensitive details stay deferred to broader parity or future matrix
review. No production or test change was required.

### Logical/physical mapping

Logical content identity, current physical mapping, stored-path identity, and
snapshot-path identity were reviewed. Store, restore, remove, read-side,
verify, repair, and GC interactions were clarified as the current distributed
mapping contract. No production or test change was required.

### Placement and restore-plan assumptions

Chunk identity, `file_chunk` ordering, legacy placement, packed placement, and
restore descriptor/recipe behavior were reviewed. Verify placement expectations
and placement-adjacent read-side exposure were also clarified. No production or
test change was required.

### Reachability and GC eligibility

Current/live roots, snapshot roots, snapshot-retained data, packed/legacy
reachability, dry-run/live GC assumptions, and repair/refcount refusal
boundaries were reviewed. The current safety model was clarified without
changing behavior. No production or test change was required.

### SQLite/PostgreSQL parity

SQLite-first direction was confirmed not to mean SQLite-only, and PostgreSQL
compatibility remains mandatory. Backend selection/configuration, schema and
migration parity, dialect handling, transaction behavior, FK behavior,
ordering, grouping, aggregation, and path comparison concerns were reviewed.
Broader matrix-shaped parity topics remain deferred. No production or test
change was required.

### Test coverage and Phase 8 guard decision

Existing catalog/backend evidence was classified as sufficient for v1.13.6.
No new Phase 8 tests were justified, and candidate guard dispositions were
recorded. The remaining backend-sensitive topics stay intentionally deferred
instead of being turned into narrow v1.13.6 guards. No production or test
change was required.

## Deferred items

The following items remain visible but are not treated as v1.13.6 blockers at
this phase:

- PostgreSQL-only live GC mutation semantics
- packed/legacy aggregate parity questions
- snapshot/read-side text-filter parity concerns
- read-side stats/inspect matrix-shaped parity
- future matrix-style backend parity expansion
