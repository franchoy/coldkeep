## Frozen Store repair contract

Repair remains copy-on-write when logical identity, the complete ordered
recipe, chunk identity, and physical placement/accounting are trustworthy.
Pre-existing `COMPLETED` and `ABORTED` chunks may participate; ambiguous or
incomplete placement and unsupported statuses fail closed.

`file_chunk.chunk_order` is the sole authoritative logical recipe order and is
never mutated. The existing schema-v17 `store_repair_chunk.chunk_order` is
only a dense attempt-local staging ordinal. Implementation may derive its
deterministic in-memory ordering from each distinct chunk's first recipe
occurrence, but staging order is not replacement recipe authority.

```text
FILE_CHUNK_RECIPE_ORDER=SOLE_AUTHORITATIVE_ORDER
STORE_REPAIR_CHUNK_CARDINALITY=ONE_ROW_PER_DISTINCT_STAGED_CHUNK
REPEATED_RECIPE_OCCURRENCES=VALIDATED_BUT_NOT_DUPLICATED_IN_STAGING
SUCCESSFUL_REPAIR_LOGICAL_RETRY_INCREMENT=EXACTLY_ONE
SUCCESSFUL_REPAIR_CHUNK_RETRY_INCREMENT=EXACTLY_ONE_PER_DISTINCT_EXISTING_CHUNK_ACTUALLY_REPAIRED
UNCHANGED_EXISTING_CHUNK_RETRY_INCREMENT=ZERO
NEW_CHUNK_RETRY_INCREMENT=ZERO
FAILED_OR_ROLLED_BACK_REPAIR_RETRY_INCREMENT=ZERO
```

A chunk is actually repaired only when successful publication changes its
authoritative status or placement. `ABORTED` to `COMPLETED` and replacement of
an active placement each qualify. Recipe membership or temporary traversal
alone does not. Repeated occurrences never multiply staging, retirement,
replacement, or retry increments. All authority and counter changes occur in
the bounded publication transaction.

If the complete authoritative recipe is missing, Store must fail without
additional catalog or physical mutation, path attachment, or reconstruction
of ownership. That fail-closed case does not broaden to trustworthy physical
or semantic corruption.

## Frozen sealing-recovery contract

Before changing sealing or quarantine state, recovery observes container
occupancy as the union of active legacy extents, retired legacy extents, and
packed `storage_blocks` extents. Packed companion `blocks` rows are excluded
from the legacy set.

The extents must be valid, disjoint, and cover exactly
`[ContainerHdrLen, current_size)`. Exact coverage may seal. A gap, overlap,
invalid extent, or trailing unowned bytes quarantines the container and clears
`sealing` through the existing safe recovery semantics. Recovery must not
truncate bytes, invent ownership, or damage unrelated authoritative data. An
occupancy-read failure returns before mutation.
