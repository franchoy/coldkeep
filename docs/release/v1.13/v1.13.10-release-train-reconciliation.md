## Original proposed v1.13.x continuation

The historical planning allocations were: v1.13.10 — Repair and Recovery
Boundary Decision; v1.13.11 — SQLite/PostgreSQL Compatibility Gate Hardening;
v1.13.12 — CLI Thin-Wrapper Proof and Remaining Coupling Review; and v1.13.13
— v1.x / v2.0 Handoff Gate. They were planning allocations, not immutable
release commitments.

## How v1.13.9 expanded

Beyond snapshot-mutation routing, v1.13.9 incorporated the repair/recovery
boundary decision and honesty proof, backend-posture and CLI-coupling
inventory work, and the v1.x/v2.0 readiness gate. Its final review classified
remaining substantive work as non-blocking v2.x work, so no separate
v1.13.10–v1.13.13 release sequence remained necessary.

## Current authoritative release sequence

### v1.13.9

`Engine Boundary Closure and v2.0 Handoff Readiness` is released and closed.
Its final verdict is `READY WITH NON-BLOCKING DEFERRALS`. It completed
sufficient boundary closure and honest classification for v2.0 readiness, not
every possible future hardening activity.

### v1.13.10

`v1.x Closure Integrity and CI Runtime Hygiene` is active and unreleased. It
owns release-state consistency, release-train truthfulness, engine-contract
documentation truthfulness, release-state validator work, CI runtime
maintenance, and v1.x closure/v2.0 handoff freeze. It is the planned final
v1.x closure release unless a blocking defect requires a corrective follow-up.

### v2.0

v2.0 is the next architecture train after v1.13.10 closes. This phase does
not start it. It inherits v1.x correctness contracts and owns the early design
decisions explicitly assigned below.

## Remaining early-v2.0 deferrals

- `StoreRequest` narrowing or contract split.
- Snapshot list/show/stats/diff read-side ownership decision.
- Design of any future repair/recovery activation.

## Remaining later-v2.x deferrals

- Broader SQLite/PostgreSQL compatibility-proof strengthening.
- Broader thin-wrapper, routing, or architectural cleanup.
- Local daemon, API, UI, scheduling/automation, and broader local
  productization work.

## Beyond-local boundary

Network or LAN service, NAS or multiple storage roots, cloud/object storage,
distributed repositories, replication/synchronization, cross-machine
coordination, centralized PostgreSQL production/server mode, and multi-user
authorization are v3.x or later scope. They do not enter local-first v2.x
without an explicit roadmap revision.

## v1.x runtime-remediation conclusion

The v1.13.9 handoff review identified no release blockers and no remaining
v1.x follow-ups. Remaining backend-proof, coupling, corrective-boundary, and
local-productization work is explicitly early-v2.0 or later-v2.x scope;
beyond-local work is v3.x or later scope. No additional v1.x runtime
implementation release is required merely to satisfy superseded
v1.13.10–v1.13.13 numbering.

This conclusion does not assert that Coldkeep has no defects and does not
prevent a future corrective v1.x release if a blocking defect is discovered.

## Change-control rule

Historical release proposals remain historical. Current release definitions
must not silently reuse old scope. When planned numbers are retired, completed,
superseded, and deferred portions must be recorded separately; this artifact
and the current train must be updated together. Released v1.13.9 evidence must
not be rewritten to match later planning.

## Final reconciliation verdict

`RECONCILED WITH EXPLICIT NON-BLOCKING DEFERRALS`

The original v1.13.10–v1.13.13 release numbers are retired. v1.13.9 completed
the required decisions, inventories, and handoff-readiness review needed to
close the v1.x runtime train, while explicitly deferring substantive
backend-proof, read-side coupling, corrective-boundary activation, and broader
productization work to v2.x. Active v1.13.10 owns closure integrity and CI
runtime hygiene.
