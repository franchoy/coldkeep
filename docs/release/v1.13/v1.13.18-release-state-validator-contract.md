## Exact source and topology rules

M must have the exact tree and ordered parents in the selected reference. A
candidate must descend from M through a merge-free preparation range. During
development its cumulative delta may be a subset of the allowlist; the Phase 2
checkpoint and every later candidate must equal all 33 status/mode/path rows.
No deletion, rename, copy, type change, symlink, submodule, dependency change,
or v1.13.17 frozen-file mutation is accepted.

A release PR must bind base `main@M` and the exact selected candidate. The
protected main result must have ordered parents `[M,R18]` and the R18 tree. A
tag event must identify a one-level annotated `v1.13.18` tag whose direct
commit target is that exact protected merge. Shared event checks also reject
forced/deleted events, foreign repositories, wrong refs, shallow repositories,
grafts, replacements, and alternate object stores.

## Diagnostic v5 and authority boundary

The selected route emits `coldkeep-release-state-diagnostic/v5` and reports
`final_snapshot_contract_schema` plus structural status. Diagnostics and
validator success remain non-authorizing. They never establish authentication,
hosted certification, protected integration, tag authority, publication,
cleanup, final owner acceptance, or historical all-success.

The closed v1.13.17 `coldkeep-v1-security-toolchain-progression-reference/v5`
grammar remains historical and unchanged. This contract defines no v6, H7,
arbitrary-descendant rule, or mixed-selector fallback.
