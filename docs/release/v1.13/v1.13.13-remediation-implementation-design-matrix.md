## Pinned operating-system binding decision

`golang.org/x/sys v0.38.0` is already pinned and unchanged. Its inspected
source provides:

- Linux `Renameat2`, `RENAME_NOREPLACE`, `Linkat`, and `Renameat`.
- Darwin `RenameatxNp`, `RENAME_EXCL`, `Linkat`, and `Renameat` on amd64 and
  arm64.
- Windows `NtCreateFile`, `NtSetInformationFile`, `OBJECT_ATTRIBUTES`,
  `IO_STATUS_BLOCK`, reparse/create/share/access constants, `DELETE`,
  `FileRenameInformation`, `FileDispositionInformation`, and replacement
  flags.

The Windows rename structure is the one narrow internal native representation
approved by Phase 7. No package upgrade, dependency, syscall generator, or
alternate binding remains for Phase 8 to choose.
