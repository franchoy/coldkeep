## 9. Final explicit bounds

### Restore

- Planned exact destinations remain exact.
- No-overwrite publication is atomic and non-replacing.
- Overwrite is intentional and explicit.
- Retained parent/object identity prevents replacement redirection within the
  frozen concurrent threat contract.
- Arbitrary hostile relocation of an already-open Unix parent is outside the
  frozen guarantee.
- Metadata truth is reported after publication; Windows projection remains
  platform-specific.

### Coordination

- Cooperative same-process/same-host/local-filesystem scope only.
- Native Linux, macOS, and Windows coordination.
- PostgreSQL advisory ownership where applicable.
- No network-filesystem or distributed-coordination guarantee.

### Backends and verification

- SQLite and PostgreSQL compatibility are supported within documented
  backend-specific differences.
- SQLite-default productization is not a v1 requirement.
- Deep verification covers authoritative legacy, packed, and mixed placement
  and fails closed on missing or conflicting authority.

## 17. V2 handoff principle

V2 begins from a complete v1 foundation. It may productize and extend but may
not silently weaken, relabel, or require repair of proven v1 semantics. The
[handoff scope matrix](v1.13.13-v2-handoff-scope-matrix.md) is the authoritative
entry classification.

## 18. V2 owned scope

V2 owns SQLite-default repository-local productization; one local daemon and
mutation owner; mutation queues and maintenance barriers; daemon-backed CLI;
local API and UI; scheduling; catalog export/import/backup; local recovery
workflows; normal-user SQLite live GC; and retention of PostgreSQL
compatibility. These are product and lifecycle extensions, not v1 fixes.

## 19. V2 inherited guarantees

V2 must preserve:

- content-addressed identity and deterministic restore;
- integrity verification and safe bounded GC;
- Engine, Catalog, composition, and thin-CLI direction;
- applicable SQLite/PostgreSQL compatibility;
- repository format and storage compatibility unless a separately governed
  migration changes it;
- fail-closed behavior; and
- immutable historical v1 release evidence.

`V2_INHERITED_GUARANTEES: FROZEN`.
