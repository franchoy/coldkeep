## Known blocker family

`RESTORE_INSTALL_CONTRACT` remains unresolved:

- With overwrite disabled, final installation is not atomic against a
  destination created in the final existence-check-to-rename window.
- An exact destination planned by snapshot or stored-path restore may be
  reinterpreted as `<directory>/<originalName>` when the exact destination
  currently exists as a directory.

These are one root contract: final restore destination semantics must be
authoritative and atomic. Phase 0 does not remediate it.
