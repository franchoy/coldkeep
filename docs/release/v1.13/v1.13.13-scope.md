# Coldkeep v1.13.13 Scope

**Release:** `v1.13.13 — Final v1.x and v2 Handoff Gate`
**Status:** Active
**Branch:** `release/v1.13.13`

## Objective

Perform an independent final v1.x audit, remediate genuine blockers only,
complete release validation, and make the formal v2 handoff decision.

## Starting baseline

`fd396cd0c8cf43662881211b8e6b2877eb9a8010`, the exact released v1.13.12
final-main and annotated-tag target.

## Current phase

Phase 2 is Complete. Phase 3 is Next.

Phase 2 independently finds Engine and thin-CLI architecture complete and
application composition narrow. Catalog planning ownership and adoption are
complete, but Catalog architecture is partial because five aggregate methods
lack the stable typed-error boundary. The reliable audit therefore passes with
zero architecture blockers while carrying `P2-ARCH-001` to Phase 6. This does
not resolve correctness, backend equivalence, coordination, restore
installation, or v1 closure.

## Current strategic result

```text
V1.13.13_MODE:
AUDIT_PLUS_REMEDIATION
```

The independent pre-branch audit confirmed v1.13.12 completion while proving
that v1.13.13 cannot remain audit-only.

## Known blocker entering the train

`RESTORE_INSTALL_CONTRACT` is unresolved. Final restore destination semantics
must be authoritative and atomic: no-overwrite installation must close the
final existence-check-to-rename race, and an exact planned destination must
not be reinterpreted as a directory destination by a lower restore primitive.
Phase 0 records but does not remediate this blocker.

## Frozen non-goals

- No new feature work.
- No v2 daemon, API, or UI.
- No SQLite-default productization.
- No NAS, network, cloud, or distributed coordination.
- No Engine or Catalog redesign without a newly proven blocker.
- No storage-format or schema migration unless a genuine blocker unexpectedly
  proves one necessary.

SQLite-default local productization remains v2.x scope. NAS, network,
distributed, and cross-host coordination remain v3.x scope. PostgreSQL
compatibility remains required.

## Closure rule

v1.x can be `APPROVED` only after every genuine blocker is closed and complete
local and hosted evidence is green. v1.x remains open and v2.x implementation
is not authorized before that decision.
