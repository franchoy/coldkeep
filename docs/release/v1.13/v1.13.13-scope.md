## Frozen non-goals

- No new feature work.
- No v2 daemon, API, or UI.
- No SQLite-default productization.
- No NAS, network, cloud, or distributed coordination.
- No Engine or Catalog redesign without a newly proven blocker.
- No storage-format or schema migration unless a genuine blocker unexpectedly
  proves one necessary.

SQLite-default local productization remains v2.x scope. NAS, network,
distributed, and cross-host coordination remain v3.x scope. PostgreSQL
compatibility remains required.

## Closure rule

v1.x can be `APPROVED` only after the remaining local and hosted release gates
are green and Phase 13 makes the formal decision. Technical blocker closure is
necessary but does not itself declare v1.x closed. v1.x remains formally open
and v2.x implementation is not authorized before that decision.
