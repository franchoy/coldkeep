## Known blocker entering the train

`RESTORE_INSTALL_CONTRACT` is unresolved. Final restore destination semantics
must be authoritative and atomic: no-overwrite installation must close the
final existence-check-to-rename race, and an exact planned destination must
not be reinterpreted as a directory destination by a lower restore primitive.
Phase 0 records but does not remediate this blocker.

- No SQLite-default productization.
- No NAS, network, cloud, or distributed coordination.
- No Engine or Catalog redesign without a newly proven blocker.

distributed, and cross-host coordination remain v3.x scope. PostgreSQL
compatibility remains required.
