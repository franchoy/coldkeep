## Scope methodology and evidence standard

Every numbered row was re-read against its exported or documented contract,
current implementation, observable caller/operator consequence, v1 defect
boundary, existing tests, and future closure-proof requirement. Passing tests
that merely reproduce the defective projection were not treated as closure.
Symptoms sharing one violated contract were deduplicated; distinct contracts
were not merged merely because they share a file.

A row is confirmed only when current evidence establishes all of contract,
implementation failure, observable consequence, v1 correction boundary, and
a feasible later proof. Phase 1 freezes classifications and owners only. It
does not choose Phase 2 designs or implement any correction.
