## Accepted finding

CK-V11316-015 is an S1 correctness finding:

`FAILED_DUPLICATE_STORE_CAN_IRREVERSIBLY_INVALIDATE_EXISTING_RECIPE`

When an existing completed object temporarily loses required physical data, a
duplicate Store may fail after changing that object's status/retry state and
deleting its recipe. Returning the exact physical bytes then cannot restore the
original mapping. A failed repair must never worsen the old authoritative
graph.

CK-V11316-014 remains an external proposed S2 and is not repository-confirmed
or included in repository totals. CK-V11316-007 remains open and unchanged.

## Frozen implementation contract

Repair-on-Store remains required for an invalid `COMPLETED` candidate whose
logical identity and ordered recipe are intact and trustworthy. Repair is
copy-on-write from the perspective of authoritative recoverability:

```text
STAGED_CONTAINER_PHYSICAL_PATH=FINAL_IMMUTABLE_CONTAINER_PATH
PUBLICATION_FILESYSTEM_RENAME=NONE
PUBLICATION_FILESYSTEM_COPY=NONE
PUBLICATION_FILESYSTEM_DELETE=NONE
PUBLICATION_AUTHORITY_CHANGE=DATABASE_TRANSACTION_ONLY
RETIRED_LEGACY_EXTENT_IDENTITY=PHYSICAL_CONTAINER_AND_OFFSET
CK015_REPAIR_RECIPE=VALIDATED_BUT_NOT_REPLACED
FILE_CHUNK_DURING_PREPARATION=UNCHANGED
FILE_CHUNK_DURING_PUBLICATION=UNCHANGED
```

Schema v17 adds durable attempt-owned staging and retired physical-accounting
state. Staged container bytes are written, synced, closed, directory-synced,
sealed, and validated at their final immutable pathname while the container is
quarantined and unreferenced. One bounded database transaction is the sole
authority switch. Old physical extents remain accounted for until safe
whole-block or whole-container collection.

For every retained packed block, encoded membership must equal active plus
retired membership exactly. Container payload occupancy must equal the
disjoint ordered union of active legacy extents, retired legacy extents, and
packed storage-block extents. Historical packed chunk IDs and legacy block or
chunk IDs are opaque provenance values, never current ownership.

The existing OS-backed exclusive repository lease excludes another compliant
process's recovery for the complete repair. PostgreSQL publication also shares
GC's advisory exclusion, locks and revalidates the complete authoritative
fingerprint, and requires zero affected Restore pins. Any material difference
aborts without changing the old graph.
