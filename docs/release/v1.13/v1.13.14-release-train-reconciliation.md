The sole current authority is [the v1.13.x release train](v1.13.x-release-train.md).

- v1.13.13 is released and operationally closed; its Phase 16 record contains
  the authoritative tag, tag-CI, and stable-publication evidence.
- v1.13.14 is active and owns corrective revalidation of the frozen 17
  findings and 4 process gates. Phase 1 confirmed the complete numbered scope,
  froze the rejection and owner-phase registers, and changed no runtime or
  release governance behavior.
- Phases 0–11 are Complete. Phase 2 froze implementation designs, regression
  contracts, compatibility impacts, and owner-phase source/test allowlists for
  all 21 rows without closing any row. Phase 3 added the five frozen,
  nil-by-default deterministic seam families without remediating or closing a
  row. Phase 4 used the verification observer to replace catalog-derived
  counts and closed only `CK-V1-AUD-001`. Phase 5 propagated caller context
  through every frozen affected operation and closed only `CK-V1-AUD-002`.
  Phase 6 centralized exactly-once single-file Store finalization, closed only
  `CK-V1-AUD-007` plus historical root-equivalent `CK-110-1728`. Phase 7
  preserved rollback truncate/close failures, retained poisoned identity until
  durable quarantine, and closed only `CK-V1-AUD-008`. Phase 8 preserved
  snapshot restore overwrite through the Engine adapter, replaced the weaker
  restore selector builder with the shared Show/Diff/Restore normalizer, and
  closed only `CK-V1-AUD-006` and `CK-V1-AUD-009`. Phase 9 applied SnapshotDiff
  Limit after query/change filtering with truthful raw/matched/summary
  populations and carried persisted snapshot membership through existing
  Catalog projections into list/show/graph/tree metadata without N+1 queries,
  closing only `CK-V1-AUD-010` and `CK-V1-AUD-012`. Its first candidate
  `3ae9558` passed local validation and CodeQL but was not accepted because
  exact-head CI and Required Gate exposed one stale integration assertion that
  equated raw diff Total with filtered returned Entries. The authorized
  follow-up preserves that failure, reconciles only the integration proof, and
  binds acceptance to fresh exact-head hosted success recorded in its execution
  report. Phase 10 then froze the complete bounded scheduler, active-container
  accounting, independent transaction, truthful partial-result, physical-byte,
  filesystem-failure, and overflow contracts for `CK-V1-AUD-003` and
  `CK-V1-AUD-004` without implementation or closure. Phase 11 implemented the
  frozen bounded scheduler, exact physical-byte and active-removal accounting,
  surfaced filesystem/overflow errors, and truthful partial results, closing
  only those two GC findings. Ten of 17 findings are closed, all 4 process
  gates remain open, Phase 12 is the sole Next phase in
  `NOT_STARTED_READY_FOR_SEPARATE_AUTHORIZATION`, and Phases 13–26 are Not
  started.
- v1.x normative scope remains complete but must not be described as finally
  correctness-certified before the later corrective phases pass.
